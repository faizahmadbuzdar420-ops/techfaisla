Infinix Smart 10 Plus Review: Best Entry-Level Phone Under PKR 35,000?

If your budget is tight but you still want a big screen, a battery that lasts all day, and a phone that doesn't feel cheap in hand, the Infinix Smart 10 Plus is worth a serious look. It sits comfortably under PKR 35,000 in Pakistan and targets students, commuters, and first-time smartphone buyers.

Display & Design

The Smart 10 Plus comes with a 6.67-inch IPS LCD display running at a smooth 120Hz refresh rate — a feature you rarely find this low in the price ladder. It's not AMOLED, so blacks won't be as deep, but scrolling and everyday use feel noticeably fluid for the price.

Performance

Power comes from a Unisoc T7250 chipset paired with 4GB RAM (plus extended virtual RAM). This isn't a gaming phone — it's built for calls, WhatsApp, YouTube, social media, and light multitasking, and it handles all of that comfortably without lag in daily use.

Camera

An 8MP rear camera keeps things simple. Daylight shots come out decent for social media sharing, though don't expect much in low light — that's a fair trade-off at this price point.

Battery

This is where the Smart 10 Plus really shines: a massive 6000mAh battery with 18W fast charging support. For light-to-moderate users, this phone can comfortably stretch across two days on a single charge.

Pros

  • 120Hz display — rare at this price
  • Huge 6000mAh battery, easily 2-day use
  • PTA approved, plug-and-play on all networks
  • Solid build for the price

Cons

  • LCD, not AMOLED — lower contrast
  • Only 4GB RAM, not for heavy gaming
  • Basic single rear camera
PKR 27,000 – 35,000 (4GB/128GB)

TechFaisla Verdict

For students and everyday users who prioritize battery life and a smooth screen over gaming power, the Infinix Smart 10 Plus is one of the best entry-level picks in Pakistan right now. Compare listings on Daraz, PriceOye, or Qeemat.com.pk before buying, since prices vary by retailer and city.
