Infinix Hot 60 Pro — Best Budget Phone Under PKR 50,000?

144Hz AMOLED display, 45W fast charging aur solid daily performance — 2026 ke budget segment ka sabse zyada charcha wala phone. Yahan hamara pura honest review hai.

Introduction | Ta'aruf

Agar aap PKR 50,000 se kam budget mein ek acha smartphone dhoond rahe hain, to Infinix Hot 60 Pro is waqt Pakistan ke budget market mein sabse zyada charcha mein hai. Yeh phone PKR 42,000–46,000 ke darmiyan milta hai aur is price range mein aisi features deta hai jo pehle sirf mehnge phones mein milti thi.

Display — Sabse Bara Selling Point

Is phone ki sabse khaas baat iska 144Hz AMOLED display hai — is price range mein aisa display milna kaafi nayi baat hai. Zyadatar competitors abhi bhi purani IPS LCD screens de rahe hain, jab ke AMOLED behtar colors, deep blacks aur better outdoor visibility deta hai.

Performance

Roz mara ke kaamon — WhatsApp, YouTube, social media, halki gaming — ke liye performance smooth rehti hai. Multitasking mein bhi phone atakta nahi. Agar aap heavy mobile gamer nahin hain, to yeh phone aapki zaroorat achi tarah puri karega.

Battery aur Charging

Budget segment mein battery life ek bara concern hoti hai, lekin is phone mein fast charging (45W tak) di gayi hai jo phone ko jaldi charge kar deti hai — office ya kaam par jaane se pehle quick top-up ke liye useful.

Camera

Camera department mein yeh phone "acha" hai, "shandar" nahin — din ki roshni mein tasveerein sharp aati hain, lekin low-light performance average hai. Agar camera aapki top priority hai, to alag options dekhna behtar ho sakta hai, lekin overall package ke hisaab se yeh phone value deta hai.

Kin Logon Ke Liye Behtareen Hai?

Students jo affordable phone mein achi display chahte hain, daily users jo social media aur halki browsing karte hain, aur wo log jo AMOLED display ka experience chahte hain lekin flagship price nahi de sakte.

Kin Logon Ke Liye Nahi?

Heavy gamers jo top-tier performance chahte hain, aur photography enthusiasts jo low-light camera quality ko priority dete hain.

Final Verdict

PKR 45,000 ke aas-paas budget rakhne walon ke liye, Infinix Hot 60 Pro is waqt sabse balanced option hai — acha display, theek-thak performance, aur fast charging. Yeh phone 2026 ke budget segment mein "value for money" ka acha example hai.
