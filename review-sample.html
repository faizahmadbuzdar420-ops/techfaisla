Design aur Build Quality

Phone haath mein premium lagta hai — titanium frame ke saath weight balanced hai, aur din bhar use mein haath thakta nahi. Corners thora sharp hain, jo grip ko behtar banate hain lekin bina case ke thora slippery mehsoos hota hai.

Camera Performance

Din ki roshni mein tasveerein bohat sharp aur natural colors ke sath aati hain. Raat ke waqt bhi noise kaafi kam hai, khaas taur par main camera se. Zoom lens 5x tak reliable hai, uske baad detail thora kam hone lagta hai.

Battery aur Charging

Normal use — social media, calls, navigation — mein phone aik pura din aur thora extra chal jata hai. Charging speed acha hai, aur agar aap delivery ya field job mein hain to yeh reliability kaam aati hai.
