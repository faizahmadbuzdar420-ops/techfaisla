Design aur Build Quality

Is price range mein phone ka build quality expectation se behtar hai. Back panel curved hai jo haath mein acha grip deta hai, aur weight bhi zyada nahi lagta. Din bhar jeb mein rakhne ya use karne mein koi mushkil nahi hoti.

Camera Performance

Din ki roshni mein photos kaafi sharp aur colors natural aate hain. Is budget mein camera performance acha hai, lekin raat ke waqt thora noise nazar aata hai jo is price range mein aam baat hai.

Battery aur Performance

Normal use mein phone pura din asani se chal jata hai. Gaming ya heavy apps mein bhi phone garam nahi hota jaldi, jo daily use — jaise navigation ya calls — ke liye reliable banata hai.
