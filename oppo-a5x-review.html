A 6000mAh battery beast aimed at buyers who want all-day backup without spending big.

Verdict: If battery life is your #1 priority and you don't care much about camera bragging rights, the Oppo A5x is one of the best-value picks under PKR 50,000 in 2026.

Pros

  • Massive 6000mAh battery with 45W fast charging
  • Smooth 120Hz display for the price
  • IP65 rating — rare at this budget
  • Clean, lightweight ColorOS 15

Cons

  • Single rear camera, no ultra-wide
  • Only 4GB RAM as base variant
  • No NFC support

Who should buy this? Students, delivery riders, and everyday users who stay out all day and hate carrying a charger. The Oppo A5x trades camera versatility for battery peace of mind — and at this price, that's a fair trade.
