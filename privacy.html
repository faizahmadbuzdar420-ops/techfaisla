Privacy Policy

Yeh Privacy Policy batati hai ke TechFaisla (yeh website) apne visitors ki information kaise handle karti hai. Website use kar ke aap in shara'et se ittefaq karte hain.

Cookies

Yeh website cookies use karti hai taake user experience behtar banaya ja sake aur website ke traffic ko samjha ja sake.

Google AdSense aur Third-Party Advertising

Yeh website Google AdSense ke zariye ads dikhati hai. Google, aik third-party vendor ke tor par, cookies use karta hai taake aapko is website aur doosri websites par aapki pichli visits ke bunyad par ads dikha sake.

Google ki DART cookie users ko personalized ads dikhane ke liye estamal hoti hai, jo un ki internet par visit ki gayi websites ke bunyad par hoti hain. Users Google ads ke liye personalized advertising ko Google's Ads Settings se disable kar sakte hain.

Third-Party Privacy Policies

TechFaisla ki Privacy Policy doosre advertisers ya websites par apply nahi hoti. Hum tajweez karte hain ke aap concerned third-party ad servers ki privacy policies bhi dekhen taake unki practices ke bare mein mazeed maloomat hasil ho sakein.

Consent

Is website ko use kar ke, aap hamari Privacy Policy se ittefaq karte hain aur uski shara'et ko qabool karte hain.
