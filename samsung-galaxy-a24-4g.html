Introduction | Ta'aruf

Samsung ka naam Pakistan mein bharosay ka doosra naam samjha jata hai — aur Galaxy A24 4G isi bharosay ko budget range mein le kar aata hai. PKR 43,000 ke aas-paas milne wala yeh phone Infinix aur Redmi jaise competitors ko seedha challenge karta hai, lekin har jagah barabar khara nahi utarta.

Display

6.5 inch ka display 90Hz refresh rate ke sath aata hai — smooth scrolling ke liye theek hai, lekin competitors jo 120Hz ya 144Hz de rahe hain, un ke saamne yeh thora peeche reh jata hai. Colors aur brightness Samsung ke standard ke mutabiq acha hai.

Performance

MediaTek Helio G99 chipset roz mara ke kaamon ke liye kaafi hai — WhatsApp, browsing, social media sab smooth chalta hai. Lekin 4GB RAM aajkal ke apps ke sath thora kam mehsoos hoti hai, especially jab aap kai apps ek sath khuli rakhte hain.

AdSense in-article unit

Camera

Triple camera setup (50MP main + 5MP ultrawide + 2MP macro) din ki roshni mein achi tasveerein deta hai. Samsung ka color processing dusre budget brands se thora natural lagta hai. Low-light mein performance average hai, lekin unusable nahi.

Battery aur Charging

5000mAh battery pura din chal jati hai normal use mein. 25W charging speed competitors ke 45W se kaafi kam hai — full charge hone mein zyada waqt lagta hai, jo daily rush mein thori inconvenience ban sakti hai.

Pros

  • Samsung ka bharosemand brand aur after-sales support
  • Natural camera colors din ki roshni mein
  • Solid battery life daily use mein

Cons

  • 4GB RAM competitors ke 8GB se kam
  • 90Hz display, na ke 120Hz/144Hz
  • 25W charging dheemi hai is price range mein

Kin Logon Ke Liye Behtareen Hai?

Wo log jo Samsung brand aur after-sales service ko priority dete hain, aur jo halka-phulka daily use (calls, WhatsApp, social media) karte hain.

Kin Logon Ke Liye Nahi?

Wo log jo zyada RAM, tez display refresh rate, ya fast charging chahte hain — un ke liye Infinix Hot 60 Pro ya Redmi Note 14 Pro behtar value dete hain.

Final Verdict

Samsung Galaxy A24 4G ek safe, bharosemand choice hai un logon ke liye jo brand name ko priority dete hain. Lekin agar sirf specs aur value dekhein, to isi budget mein Infinix Hot 60 Pro aur Redmi Note 14 Pro zyada de rahe hain. Yeh phone "acha" hai, lekin is budget ka "best" nahi.
