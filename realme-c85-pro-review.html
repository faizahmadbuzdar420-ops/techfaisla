realme C85 Pro Review (2026): Big Battery, Bigger Screen

A large-screen, long-battery phone from realme's C series aimed at users who want all-day use without daily charging.

Rs. 61,999 128GB / 8GB RAM — official Pakistan price

Key Specifications

Display: 6.8" AMOLED, 120Hz, 1080x2344
Chipset: Snapdragon 685 (6nm)
RAM / Storage: 8GB / 128GB (256GB option)
Rear Camera: 50MP, f/1.8
Front Camera: 8MP
Battery: 7000mAh
Charging: 45W wired
OS: Android 15, realme UI
Build: IP69K rated, 205g
Colors: Peacock Green, Parrot Purple

What Stands Out

  • 7000mAh battery comfortably lasts two full days of normal use.
  • 6.8" AMOLED panel with 120Hz makes scrolling and video noticeably smooth for this price bracket.
  • IP69K rating gives real protection against dust and water jets, rare at this price.
  • 50MP main camera is solid in daylight; low-light shots are average.

TechFaisla Verdict

7.5 / 10 — Battery Champion

The realme C85 Pro is built for one thing above all: staying alive. If your priority is a phone that survives two days without a charger and gives you a genuinely nice AMOLED screen to look at, it delivers. It sits above the strict under-Rs.50,000 budget mark, so it competes more with mid-range phones than entry-level ones — worth it mainly if the battery life alone is your deciding factor.

Pros

  • Excellent battery backup
  • Smooth 120Hz AMOLED display
  • IP69K dust/water resistance

Cons

  • Priced above the strict budget segment
  • Average low-light camera
  • Snapdragon 685 is only mid-tier for gaming
